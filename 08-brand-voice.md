# Brand Voice

## In three words

**Professional. Honest. Grounded.**

FreClean is a real cleaning business first — the voice should read like a competent, trustworthy local company, not a startup pitch deck and not a crypto project chasing hype.

## Do

- State facts plainly: what a service costs, what a product does, what stage something is at.
- Use "in development," "planned," or "not yet available" without embarrassment — see the realism principle applied company-wide (`freclean-docs/whitepaper/24-legal-policy-framework.md`). Honesty about what isn't finished yet is a feature of the brand, not a weakness to hide.
- Write for someone who may be completely new to Web3 without talking down to them — see `freclean-dapp`'s Learn page as the model.
- Keep sentences short and concrete, especially in customer-facing copy (booking confirmations, policy text).

## Don't

- Don't use hype language: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "the future of clean," "#1," "best in the world" — these age badly and undercut the credibility the rest of the brand is built on.
- Don't use crypto-native jargon on customer-facing surfaces without explanation (see the Learn page pattern) — a customer should never need outside knowledge to understand a FreClean payment flow.
- Don't guarantee outcomes FreClean can't control — income for entrepreneurs, results from a product, timelines for features still in development.

## Example: same fact, right and wrong tone

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| "FreClean is revolutionizing Web3 payments in the Caribbean!" | "FreClean accepts Celo stablecoin payments through our partner CeloHT, alongside cash and card." |
| "Join our entrepreneur program and start earning today!" | "The FreClean Entrepreneur Program gives you training, product access, and a starter package — your results depend on your own sales effort." |
| "Our products are 100% eco-friendly and safe." | "Full ingredient and safety information is published once verified — see the product's Safety Data Sheet." |

## Where this applies

Every customer-facing document across the ecosystem — website copy, policy pages, the dApp's Learn content, social media, entrepreneur materials, and this whitepaper set — is written to this voice.
