# Packaging Guidelines

Product packaging label requirements (what must appear on a bottle, what not to print until verified) are maintained in `freclean-products/docs/PACKAGING-GUIDELINES.md`, not duplicated here, so the two don't drift out of sync.

## What belongs here instead

The brand-level rules packaging must follow regardless of product:

- Logo reproduction on packaging must meet the minimum size and clear-space rules in `01-logo-usage.md`.
- Packaging color use should draw from the approved palette (`02-color-system.md`) — the current label concept uses FreClean Blue and Green in a gradient consistent with the "wipe" motif.
- Typography on packaging uses the same three-typeface system (`03-typography.md`); product name in Space Grotesk, fine print in Inter, batch/lot codes in IBM Plex Mono once batch tracking is enabled.

See `freclean-products/docs/PACKAGING-GUIDELINES.md` for label content requirements, and `freclean-products/assets/img/` for current packaging photography.
